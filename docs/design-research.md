# Door design research — July 2026

Basis for the Doorswala catalog artwork. Sourced from Pinterest trend pages and design
publications describing the most-pinned door styles of 2025–26, plus Indian main-door
design guides. All catalog artwork is **original SVG recreated from these motifs** —
no photographs are copied.

## Trend motifs collected

| Motif | Detail | Source trend |
|---|---|---|
| Fluted / reeded texture | Continuous vertical half-round flutes across the leaf | 2026 interior trend ("fluted textures", "decorative grooves") |
| Vertical brass inlay | 1–2 slim full-height brass lines on dark wood | Indian main-door trend ("vertical brass or SS-304 inlays") |
| Japandi slats | Narrow timber slats, shadow gaps, pale oak, minimal pull | Pinterest "Japandi door" idea page |
| Jali lattice | Geometric wooden lattice over frosted backing — airflow + privacy | Indian jali door trend |
| Art-deco marquetry | Sunburst / fan veneer in two tones with brass ribs | Statement-door trend |
| Chevron panelling | Diagonal two-tone veneer chevrons | "Chevron pattern door" |
| Ribbed glass insert | Vertical ribbed/frosted glass, black grid frame | 2026 glass-insert trend ("frosted, ribbed", "black grid framing") |
| Matte black statement | Flat black leaf, long brass pull | "Bold front door colors" +156% YoY on Pinterest; matte black hardware |
| Sage / bold color | Painted fluted leaf in sage green | 2026 color trend (sage, navy) |
| Carved heritage | Temple/fort-inspired carved borders, aged brass studs | "Fort-inspired", hand-carved teak trend |
| Book-matched two-tone | Split-face color blocking with metal seam | "Double laminated" evolution, color-block trend |
| Industrial steel | Brushed graphite, vertical seams, smart-lock plate | Safety + industrial aesthetic |
