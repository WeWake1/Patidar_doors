# Reference sites (round 1, 23 Nov 2026)

The owner found no door, timber or furniture site worth being mistaken for, asked for "crazy" ones, and named
Apple's product pages among the best (decisions, round 1 Q7). These are for reacting to: what to steal, what to
avoid. Each has two captures, 1440 px: `-1` the first screen, `-2` after some scrolling.

**Use the technique, never the look.** None of these sells doors; the brief's rule for Pallet Euro applies here too.

## Crazy

| Site | What it is | Worth taking | Captures |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Oryzo](https://oryzo.ai/) by Lusion | A cork coaster presented, deadpan, like a frontier AI product: "the world's most unnecessarily sophisticated cork coaster". | The closest analogy to our problem: a humble physical object made unforgettable by treating it with absurd seriousness. Doors and timber are our cork coaster. | `oryzo-1/2.webp` |
| [Igloo Inc](https://www.igloo.inc/) by Bureaux with Abeto | A world built of ice blocks that you scroll through. Awwwards Site of the Year and Developer Site of the Year 2024. | One material as the entire world. Ours would be timber. | `igloo-inc-1/2.webp` |
| [Lusion](https://lusion.co/) | The studio's own site: tactile 3D objects that react to the cursor. | Physical, toy-like interaction that still feels expensive. | `lusion-1/2.webp` |
| [Messenger](https://messenger.abeto.co/) by Abeto | A tiny planet you explore. | A complete little world you can hold in one screen. | `messenger-abeto-1/2.webp` |
| [Bruno Simon](https://bruno-simon.com/) | A portfolio you drive around in a toy car. | The far end of "crazy". Probably too far for a store, but it proves play holds attention. | `bruno-simon-1/2.webp` |

## Premium storytelling (the bar the owner named)

| Site | What it is | Worth taking | Captures |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Apple · AirPods Pro](https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/) | One product, one fact per screen, the render doing the talking. | The rhythm: a single true thing per beat, shown rather than claimed. | `apple-airpods-pro-1/2.webp` |
| [Apple · iPhone](https://www.apple.com/iphone/) (the iPhone 17 Pro address now redirects here) | A range of products presented without a grid of identical cards. | How to show a range of doors and still feel premium. | `apple-iphone-pro-1/2.webp` |
| [Shopify Editions](https://www.shopify.com/editions) | Each release is an object on a shelf. | The catalogue as a physical display, like a store wall. | `shopify-editions-1/2.webp` |
